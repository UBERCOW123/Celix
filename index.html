04 Work

Selected work.

Blockchain voting on Ethereum

A proof-of-concept voting system written in C++, exploring decentralised and transparent voting. A command-line client talks JSON-RPC to an Ethereum node, reads candidates and tallies from a smart contract, casts votes, and issues certificates as proof of participation.

Language
C++
Libraries
libcurl, nlohmann/json
