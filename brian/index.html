How it works

One line in. The filing gets done.

01

You type. That's the whole decision.

Other tools make you pick a list, a tag, or a file before you write. Brian charges nothing at the moment you capture.

02

The filing happens after.

A task with a time, a number to track, something to watch, or just a thought. The day, the projects, and the habits come out of lines you would have typed anyway.

03

It stays yours.

The database is a file on the device. You can export the whole thing. You choose the model, including one that runs on the phone.
