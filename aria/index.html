Product 02 / Phone

Aria

She answers the shop phone as if she works there.

Try a live call → Open Aria ↗
01 How it works 3 parts

She knows the shop. You stay in charge.

01

She learns the shop.

What you sell, when you are open, and the number customers already call.

02

You stay in charge.

Change a price, mark something sold out, or pause Aria. The next call uses it.

03

She answers first.

Say person, or press 0, and the caller reaches the team. The request lands on WhatsApp in the shop.

02 Who she works for 4 kinds of shop

Shops she already speaks for.

Restaurant and takeaway

The order, the extras, collection or delivery, then the total read back.

Salon and barber

Cut, colour, or nails, with a preferred stylist and a time. The salon confirms before it is booked.

Reservations and the counter

How many people, which day, and what time. A deli preorder works the same way. Allergy questions go to a person.

Everyone else

Opening times, the questions you get every day, and a clear message for the team.
