Email Insights

An analytical tool to optimize team workflow by highlighting communication bottlenecks.

Core Features

Workflow Analysis

Visually map out how information flows through your team to identify key communicators.

Bottleneck Detection

Automatically flag emails or threads where responses are late, a single person is overloaded.

Response Time Metrics

Track team-wide and individual average response times to ensure timely client communication.

How It Works

  1. 1

    Connect Your Inbox

    Securely grant read-only access to your team's shared inboxes or distribution lists via OAuth.

  2. 2

    Process Metadata

    Our system analyzes email metadata (sender, receiver, timestamp) without reading the body content, ensuring privacy.

  3. 3

    View Your Dashboard

    Access a simple, real-time dashboard showing your communication graph, key metrics, and actionable alerts.
