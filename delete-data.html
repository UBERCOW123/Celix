Data Deletion Instructions

Our "Email Insights" browser extension is designed with privacy as a priority. **We do not have a server, and we do not collect or store any of your personal data.**

All data generated by the extension (such as your email statistics, settings, and analysis credits) is stored **only on your local computer** using your browser's secure storage.

Because the data is only on your machine, you have full control and are the only one who can delete it. To clear all data stored by the extension, please follow these steps:

  1. In your browser, right-click the **"Email Insights" extension icon** in your toolbar.
  2. From the menu, click on **"Options"**.
  3. This will open the extension's options page. Scroll down to the bottom to find the **"Data Management"** section.
  4. Click the red button that says **"Clear All Stored Data"**.
  5. A confirmation box will appear. Click **"OK"** to confirm.

After following these steps, all statistics, analysis credits, and settings associated with the extension will be permanently erased from your computer. Uninstalling the extension will also remove all associated data.
